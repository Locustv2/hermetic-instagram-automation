# Hermetic Instagram Series — Master Visual & Layout Template Specification

Every image generated for the **Principle of Correspondence** (and future Hermetic Principles) Instagram series MUST strictly adhere to this master template so the Instagram grid looks cohesive, uniform, and immediately recognizable.

---

## 1. Color Palette (Strict Two-Tone Gold on Obsidian Monochromatic Spec)
- **Canvas Background (`#0B0C10` → `#121624`):** Matte Deep Obsidian Void (`#0B0C10`) at the outer edges with a subtle radial vignette into Midnight Cosmic Indigo (`#121624`) at the center.
- **Primary Linework & Ornamental Frame (`#D4AF37`):** Burnished Antique Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Focal Highlights & Typography (`#F3E5AB`):** Luminous Champagne Gold (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Shadow / Recess Fill (`#07080C`):** Pure Charcoal-Black inside the lower typography cartouche for maximum legibility.
- **Strictly Forbidden Colors:** No cyan, no electric blue, no purple/magenta nebulae, no warm orange/red flames, no green foliage, and no photorealistic skin tones. Every element must be rendered exclusively in antique gold (`#D4AF37` / `#F3E5AB`) over deep obsidian (`#0B0C10`).

---

## 2. Structural Layout & Grid (1:1 Square Canvas)
Every image shares the exact same 3-part architectural layout:
1. **Outer Alchemical Border (Inset 4% from all edges):**
   - Double-line rectangular frame in burnished antique gold (`#D4AF37`) with matching corner astrolabe/rosette flourishes.
2. **Upper Central Medallion (Upper 65% of canvas, dead-center symmetrical):**
   - A large circular sacred-geometry astrolabe ring (inscribed with fine degree ticks and a subtle Metatron/hexagram geometric grid) framing the central symbolic engraving.
   - Composition is strictly symmetrical along the central vertical axis.
3. **Lower Typography Cartouche (Bottom 28% of canvas):**
   - A horizontal rectangular architectural plaque (`#07080C` fill, framed by a thin double gold rule `#D4AF37` with small diamond flourishes on the left and right edges).
   - Houses the quote text in centered, balanced lines.

---

## 3. Illustration Medium & Technique
- **Style:** 17th-century Hermetic copperplate engraving and alchemical woodcut etching (inspired by Robert Fludd, Athanasius Kircher, and *The Kybalion* plates).
- **Texture:** Crisp metallic gold cross-hatching, fine linework, and delicate gold-dust stippling on dark textured archival obsidian paper.
- **Lighting:** Subtle golden rim-glow radiating from the center of the medallion; flat, uniform archival lighting across the border and cartouche.

---

## 4. Typography Specification
- **Typeface Style:** Classical Roman Engraved Serif (Trajan / Garamond / Caslon Monumental Caps).
- **Color:** Luminous Champagne Gold (`#F3E5AB`) on the `#07080C` dark cartouche background.
- **Placement:** Strictly centered inside the lower rectangular cartouche—never floating randomly over busy illustration details.

---

## 5. Master Prompt Template for Image Generation
When generating any image in this series, use the following base prompt structure:

> **Master Prompt Prefix:**
> "1:1 square Hermetic copperplate engraving in a strict two-tone palette of burnished antique gold (#D4AF37) and champagne gold (#F3E5AB) on a matte deep obsidian-black (#0B0C10) background. No blue, no cyan, no purple, no multi-colored lighting—strictly monochromatic gold etching on obsidian. Architectural layout: an outer double-line antique gold border with corner rosette flourishes; in the upper 65%, a symmetrical circular sacred-geometry astrolabe medallion framing [CENTRAL SYMBOLIC MOTIF IN GOLD COPPERPLATE LINEWORK]; in the bottom 28%, a dark obsidian rectangular architectural plaque framed in thin gold filigree containing the centered classical engraved Roman serif typography in luminous champagne gold (#F3E5AB) reading: \"[EXACT QUOTE TEXT]\"."
